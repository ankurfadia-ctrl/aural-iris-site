What is Aural Iris?

A documentation tool built by an emergency physician to help with ED notes, reasoning and medical decision-making (MDM).

How does information get in?

Let Iris listen, dictate a quick thought, paste chart details or add a chart photo.

Can I customize my ED notes?

Yes. Customize note sections, wording and workflow preferences so the note reads in your style.

Who signs the note?

You do. Review and edit, then copy the note into your EHR for your final approval and signature.
