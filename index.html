What Aural Iris does

It listens to encounters and accepts dictation, pasted text, chart photos and screenshots; integrates the information; and organizes work through a room/encounter map. The physician keeps final clinical approval and signature.

Listen and capture

Audio, dictation, pasted chart text, and permitted chart photos or screenshots, brought into one encounter workspace.

Room map

Organize work by room and encounter, with supported room labels, layout and workflow settings you can customize.

Clinical reasoning and MDM

Supports clinical reasoning, differential considerations and medical decision-making drafting for the physician to review and correct.

Medicolegal documentation

Detailed documentation support, with prompts for missing information so the physician can add actual findings and rationale.

MIPS / quality prompts

Quality-measure documentation prompts for physician review. Performance-year specifications and actual measure fulfillment still require verification.

Your note, your style

Tailor supported note structure, style, rules and workflow settings, then copy reviewed sections into the EHR.

Example workflow

IllustrativeAn example of the intended supported workflow. It contains no patient data.

  1. Choose the encounter. Open the patient’s assigned room on the map and verify the correct encounter. Customize supported room labels/layout and documentation preferences.
  2. Bring the information together. Listen or dictate; paste relevant chart text; capture permitted chart photos or screenshots. Verify readability, encounter association and capture status.
  3. Review the integrated encounter. Iris combines the supplied information into the encounter workspace and supports clinical reasoning, differential considerations and MDM drafting. Correct discrepancies and confirm the supporting facts.
  4. Address what is missing. Review relevant documentation, medicolegal and quality-measure prompts. Add actual findings, decisions and rationale; do not invent a discussion, examination, treatment or completed measure.
  5. Finish in your own style. Refine the supported note sections, wording and rules; review the entire output, then copy the approved sections into the EHR. The physician controls clinical decisions and personally approves/signs the final record.

Make the workflow yours: tailor the supported note structure, style, rules and room settings. Review the integrated information, drafts and quality prompts, then personally approve the final record. Available functions depend on the version and configuration.
