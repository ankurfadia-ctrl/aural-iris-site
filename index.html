Prompts, not decisions.

Cited patient-safety and completeness prompts are grounded in the encounter. You can accept, correct, defer or dismiss. Cited reference material and options are offered for your consideration. You decide.

FAQ

What is Iris?

Iris is an AI clinical assistant that handles documentation and chart work so clinicians can focus on their patients.

Who is Iris for?

Clinicians who document patient care: physicians, nurse practitioners (NPs) and physician assistants (PAs).

Who signs?

You do. You review, edit and verify everything, then give final approval and signature. Iris never signs and never places orders.

Who built Iris?

Built by a physician trained in emergency medicine, internal medicine and cardiology.
